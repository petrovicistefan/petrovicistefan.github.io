Affiliate disclosure

mcp-dealfinder is built to use affiliate links from third-party affiliate networks. When you buy something after clicking one of our affiliate links, the merchant may pay the publisher a commission. It does not cost you anything extra.

How links are marked

Whenever the server returns a link that can earn a commission, it returns this text with it, and the assistant is instructed to show it next to the link:

Affiliate link: if you buy through it, the publisher may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

If a link cannot be tracked, for example because no affiliate account is configured for that merchant, the server returns the plain merchant link and no disclosure.

What affiliate relationships do not change

  • Search results are ranked by relevance to the query, or by price on request. Commission rates are not used in ranking.
  • The price you pay is the merchant's price. Affiliate tracking does not add fees.
  • Product information comes from the merchant's own feed and may be out of date. Check the merchant's page before buying.

Who earns the commission

In the free version, links use the publisher's affiliate accounts. In the Pro version, a licensed user enters their own affiliate accounts, and commissions go to them under their own agreements with the networks. The disclosure text is the same in both cases.
